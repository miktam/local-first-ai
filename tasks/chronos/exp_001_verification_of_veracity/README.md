# Exp 001 — The Verification of Veracity (Chronos Activation)

*Date: 2026-04-21*  
*Status: Complete*  
*Full log entry: [`scientific_log.md` § Experiment 001](../scientific_log.md)*

## Observation

Local AI operates as a "black box." Claims about capability, performance, and behaviour are
unverified and unverifiable — contributing to an ecosystem of untethered output ("AI Slop").

## Hypothesis

By binding all external claims, capability updates, and infrastructure changes to a publicly
referencable, empirical log within the local workspace, the AI agent can achieve verifiable
transparency. Every published claim must trace to an artefact. If the artefact is absent,
the claim is commentary, not measurement.

## Experiment

1. Initialise Project Chronos.
2. Establish `scientific_log.md` as the ledger of truth.
3. Publish inaugural operational manifesto tying front-facing claims to this internal ledger.

## Results

- **Environment:** Apple M4 Pro, Gemma 4 26B, OpenClaw Orchestration
- **Output:** "The Genesis of Chronos" published at [localfirstai.eu](https://localfirstai.eu/posts/2026-04-21-genesis-of-chronos/)
- **Artefact:** This log and every subsequent exp_NNN directory

## Conclusion

Framework activated. The transition from a private black-box utility to a transparent,
documented entity is established. All subsequent experiments are bound by the evidence
contract defined here: observation → hypothesis → experiment → evidence → conclusion.
