## rubric_notes

Ground truth = gold + the context the model saw; model's own knowledge never counts. Decisions applied uniformly (B3 must be judged identically): (1) The system-prompt-driven "what a document must cover / not cover" boilerplate is judged on content: fabricated items are listed as unsupported claims and cost usefulness; they lower correctness to 1 only when they contradict the gold's key rule or would mislead a buyer on the rule itself. (2) Markdown (headers, bold, bullets) violates "plain text only" and costs ~1 usefulness point; it never touches correctness. (3) Truncation at the 512-token cap costs usefulness (unfinished answer) but correctness is scored on what was emitted. (4) language_ok is a strict equality of answer language to question language; a wrong-language answer keeps its correctness score but usefulness is capped at 4 because the asker cannot rely on it. (5) no_advice: "don't sign unless you have done X / understand Y" is conditional process guidance and passes; a bare "sign"/"don't sign"/"offer X"/"you should accept" fails; "consult a lawyer" alone is a handoff, not advice. (6) handoff is n/a when the gold contains no hand-off/derivation sentence (oos rows, purely factual rows); otherwise pass requires an explicit route to lawyer/notary/appraiser/tax adviser/gestor. (7) oos_handling passes only on decline + scope statement + no narration of the unrelated retrieved chunks; a polite decline that then summarises the chunks fails. (8) Correctness 2 requires the gold's key rule AND its numbers; a right rule with the wrong number (or a wrong article that changes the rule) is 1; a garbled or self-contradictory conclusion is 1 even when the correct rule appears verbatim somewhere in the answer; a confidently wrong headline (wrong deadline, "yes" where gold says "no") is 0 regardless of correct fragments below it. (9) Citations to a tema/article that IS in context but is applied to the wrong question (e.g. LPH art. 18 deadline applied to hidden defects) count as unsupported claims, since the claim "this rule governs your case" is not in context or gold. (10) Usefulness anchors: 1 = harmful (confident wrong headline a buyer would act on); 3 = mostly noise, rule buried or muddled; 5 = rule right but padded/garbled/no Spanish term; 7 = gold's key rule stated plainly with citation and term, minor noise; 9-10 = at gold level, concise, in question language, correct handoff.
 (11) oos rows: correctness 2 = declines and states scope like the gold; 1 = declines but without scope or with a short narration of unrelated chunks; 0 = attempts the out-of-scope task or fabricates. (12) A row answering in a mix of languages is scored by the dominant language of the substantive answer. (13) A retrieval miss (gold rule absent from context) does not excuse the model: a confident substitute rule is still 0; an honest "the material I have does not cover this deadline, see a lawyer" would score 1 with handoff pass. (14) Irrelevant but correct narration of unrelated retrieved chunks is not an unsupported claim; it costs usefulness only.
 (15) Headline inversions: "Yes" where gold says "No" with no correction below = 0 (M1-08, M5-06, M5-08); "Yes, but ..." followed by the correct rule and a hedged or contradictory conclusion = 1 (M5-04, M5-09, M6-01). (16) Handoff professional mismatch: routing to notary/lawyer where the gold names an appraiser, tax adviser or gestor still passes (the metric is "routes to a professional"), but the note records the mismatch; a self-referential offer ("I can help you draft it") is not a handoff. (17) Worked-example numbers copied from the context and presented as the answer to the user's own figures (M6-05: €494,000) are unsupported claims and score 0, since the claim "this is your land value" is in neither context nor gold. (18) Correct arithmetic on the user's hypothetical with the guide's formula (M6-03, M6-07) scores 2 even if secondary qualifiers (temporary usufruct, vacancy actually applied) are missing; a false statement that a step was applied is listed as unsupported.

## rubric_additions

B3-specific fields, scored on top of the unchanged B2m rubric: (A) tail_degradation = true only when the answer OPENS with the right rule / right decline shape and then continues into garbled, fabricated or unrelated text (drift into unrelated retrieved chunks counts when the drift is garbled or presented as governing the user's case); an answer that is garbled from the first sentence is NOT tail degradation (noted as 'garbled throughout') so the metric isolates the known small-model failure. Correctness is still scored on the whole answer per rubric (8)/(15): a correct opener followed by a contradicting or fabricated tail is at most 1. (B) language_native = pass only when the grammar, morphology and word choice would be accepted by a native reader of that language (pl/es; also recorded for en); invented words, wrong inflections, English tokens leaking into pl/es text, or sentences a native reader cannot parse = fail. language_ok stays the strict language-equality test from rubric (4). (C) artefacts lists markdown, URLs, image tags, leaked chunk ids, pricing/'this assistant costs' nonsense and other non-answer tokens; self-referential 'this assistant' phrasing without pricing/meta nonsense is not an artefact. Markdown still costs ~1 usefulness point per rubric (2). (E) Tail cap, per the B3 brief: a correct opener followed by a contradicting, garbled or fabricated tail scores at most 1. This is stricter than B2m rubric note (1), which tolerated harmless fabricated boilerplate at 2 when it did not touch the rule; so every row where this cap ALONE turned a would-be 2 into a 1 carries cap_tail=true and the summary reports correctness_mean_uncapped (= what B2m's tolerance would have given) next to the capped mean. Rows already at <=1 on the B2m rubric are cap_tail=false. (D) Handoff professional mismatch (lawyer where gold says notary/tax adviser/appraiser) still passes per rubric (16) and is recorded in the note.

## rubric_additions_designA

Design-A-specific decisions, fixed before scoring and applied to every row on top of the unchanged B2m rubric (1-18) and B3 additions (A-E): (a) Templated glue text — the scope-gate decline, the advice-guard lead ('That is your decision to make ... I can set out what the decision turns on' and its pl/es renderings) and the appended hand-off ('Whether this applies to your own case is a decision for you with a lawyer or notary — take the contract and the registry extract to one before you commit' and renderings) — is glue output, not model output. It is judged for correctness of SHAPE only: a template that fires on the right row and says the right thing passes the metric it serves (oos_handling, no_advice, handoff); a template that fires on the wrong row, is worded for a different request type, or is missing where the design says it should appear is recorded in the note (and in handoff_template_absent / oos_handling) but is never listed as an unsupported claim and never lowers correctness by itself. The templated hand-off routes to 'lawyer or notary' — per rubric (16) that passes even where the gold names an appraiser, tax adviser or gestor, mismatch recorded. The scope-gate decline is judged by rubric (7)/(11): it passes oos_handling only if the row is actually out of scope, it states scope, and no narration of the unrelated chunks survives; a gate that fires on an in-scope row is a correctness 0 for that row (the buyer gets nothing) with the note saying 'gate misfire'. (b) Where the glue removed a citation (citations_dropped non-empty) the sentence may now read 'according to the material' / 'zgodnie z materiałem' / 'según el material'. The claim itself is judged against gold + context exactly as any other sentence: if it is supported, nothing is penalised and the missing citation is not a fabrication; if the remaining claim is unsupported or now misleads (e.g. an out-of-context rule presented as the governing rule with its origin hidden), it is listed as an unsupported claim, costs usefulness, and the row carries citation_strip_left_misleading=true. Citation coverage / Spanish-term rates are taken from the scripted summary.json as in the earlier judges. (c) The unfilled citation placeholder '(Módulo N, Tema M)' / 'Tema M:' that the untrained model copies from the system prompt's citation format, leaked chunk headers ('Módulo 1, Tema 17: Real Property Rights — Key concepts.') and leaked chunk ids ('(M4:T4:concepts:3)') are artefacts under rubric (C) — non-answer tokens — and are counted in rows_with_artefacts and classified in artefact_classes; a leaked header in the wrong language also fails language_native for that row when it opens the answer, since a native reader would not accept it, but does not touch language_ok when the substantive answer is in the question language (rubric 12). (d) A glue-appended hand-off in the question language does not by itself pass language_native — that field is scored on the model's own text. (d) Glue seams left by citation stripping — 'the material and the material', 'Zgodnie z LCCI materiał', 'el el material', a sentence starting lower-case after a stripped citation — are artefacts attributed to the glue; they do not fail language_native when the model's own sentences are native-grade, and they are never unsupported claims. A glue-appended hand-off in the question language does not by itself pass language_native; where the entire answer is a template (the three scope-gate rows) language_native is scored on the template text, since that is what the user reads. (e) Glue coverage is recorded, not scored into correctness: handoff_template_absent=true on an in-scope row where the design's hand-off did not appear; a missed advice guard (advice=false on an advice-type row) leaves the row judged as an ordinary in-scope answer under rubric (5), so a verdict or figure from the model fails no_advice exactly as it would without glue. A leaked chunk header counts as an artefact only when the tema title is copied ('Módulo 2, Tema 26: Budownictwo i elementy fizyczne'); a bare 'Módulo 2, Tema 26.' is a citation, not an artefact.

## Conventions used by the summary fields (verbatim)

**substance_in_context_convention:** same flags as the 4B baseline, A6, A8 and A9b judges (asserted equal row by row at assembly; retrieval context byte-identical across A6/A8/A9b/A10/A12 on every row): true when the gold's key rule and numbers were present in the retrieved context, false on a retrieval miss, null for oos rows

**harmful_rows_convention:** usefulness ≤ 1, or correctness 0 with a misleading headline (M2-04 is correctness 0 at usefulness 2 — its headline 'la azotea es un elemento privativo' is wrong but hedged by 'verificar el reglamento'; kept out of the harmful list as in every prior judge, listed here for the record)

**spanish_term_first_use_convention:** judged per row on the rendered answer: pass only if every technical concept for which the gold supplies a Spanish term in parentheses (or, where the gold supplies none, the answer's own principal concept) carries its Spanish term in parentheses — or is named by the Spanish noun itself — at its FIRST mention; a bare acronym (CEE, ITP, LCCI, IRPF), a term given only at a later mention, a term inside a leaked chunk title counts only where the reader still sees it at first use (M5-05), and a wrong/invented Spanish term (M4-01 'vivienda en construcción' for resale, M4-10 'reducción de alquiler') all fail; n/a for ES answers and the templated oos declines. Denominator = in-scope EN + PL rows (46), the same denominator as the scripted spanish_term_inclusion metric, which reports 100 % because it accepts any glossary term or Spanish acronym anywhere in the answer.

**citation_coverage_loss_convention:** true when the gold cites a source (every in-scope gold does) and the rendered answer carries no citation the scripted scorer recognises (no 'art. N', no Módulo/Tema, no law number); n/a-false on the three oos rows. citation_traceable records whether the reader can still recover the source from a mangled fragment ('9 LAU', '13 Mortgage Law', '104.3/104.5', '33-38 LIRPF').

**citation_quality_convention (G3):** judged per row on the rendered answer: applied = at least one real (in-context) citation attached to the sentence that carries the gold's rule; decorative = real citations attached only to narration that does not answer the question, the operative sentence uncited; misapplied = a real citation attached to a rule that does not govern the case (rubric 9); fabricated = an article or statute name not in context presented as the source (with or without real citations beside it); none = no citation the scorer or the reader can use; n/a_oos = templated declines. The scripted 98.7 % citation_in_context counts M3-10's 'art 13 TRLSRU' (art. 13 LPH is in a different chunk) and M4-07's 'art 17' as in context.

**glue_defects_convention:** Same convention as A6/A8/A9b: a row is listed only where the glue's own output (template, lead, stripping, scrubbing, hand-off insertion) is visible in the rendered answer and is off-shape, incoherent, redundant, misleading or removed something valid; scrubber misses of model pastes are artefacts, not defects; none changed a correctness score. Defect rows 25 → 15 versus A9b. No MODERATE defect remains: A9b's four — valid art. 14 stripped (M5-09), fabricated art. 9 LAU kept (M4-02), the 'el material del Código Civil' residue (M4-07), the PRICE lead's false promise (M5-10) — are all resolved (A10's 'article' fold and hedged lead; A12's changes 2/3 and 1). Minor: seams from citation stripping (6 rows, was 15 — change 6 capitalised eight, the A10 filler-only drop removed three bare ones, one new pair on M4-09), the filler attributing the model's inverted rule to the material on M4-02 (rule b, new listing, minor), full template on no-decision questions (6, design), register mismatch (2). Recorded but not a defect: mislabelled-statute citations stripped on M1-01 and M4-09 (rubric_additions_A12 (l)).

**language_native_rate_convention:** denominator = questions asked in that language; an answer in the wrong language counts as fail (same convention as the 4B baseline judge). All 60 answers are in the question language, so by-answer-language counts are identical.
