## (a) Recomputation

**Results table.** Every cell reproduces; no mismatch. 360 judged answers over 90 rows, complete and unique. The harmful flags obey the rubric convention.

**H2a: +0.2000, CI [+0.050, +0.375], n = 40, reproduced exactly.** The superiority reading depends on the method:
- seeds 1 and 7 give the same interval; seed 2026 gives [+0.050, +0.350];
- unstratified paired bootstrap: [+0.025, +0.375];
- paired t: [+0.020, +0.380];
- 9 rows up, 2 down, 29 unchanged;
- sign test p = 0.065; exact sign-flip permutation p = 0.056 (exp_038's primary test);
- bootstrap p(mean ≤ 0) = 0.0085; p(mean ≤ −0.10) = 0.0001.

Strata sizes are 8, 7, 5, 4, 4, 4, 3, 2, 2, 1, so the stratified interval is the narrowest of the reasonable choices. **Non-inferiority passes under every method. Superiority is suggestive, not shown.**

**Harmful, 13 → 7: reproduced.**
- 6 rows harmful in both arms, 7 fixed, 1 new (M4-06). McNemar exact p = 0.070.
- Five of the 7 fixed rows are retrieval misses. On with-substance rows the change is 6 → 4; on misses, 7 → 3.

**The comparison the write-up omits: A12 − B0-r on the same 40 rows is +0.225.**
- CI [+0.05, +0.425], bootstrap p(≤ 0) = 0.013, sign-flip p = 0.078.
- A12 and B0-r carry the same weights. They differ by quantiser (Unsloth's against ours) and by glue.
- R1+G − R1 = 0.000 on all 40 rows and on all 20 v2.1 rows.
- exp_035's B0 (Unsloth quantisation, English prompt, old index) scored 1.50 on 38 rows. Against that, A12 is 1.425, R2 1.40 and B0-r 1.20.
- **The design cannot separate "training raised correctness" from "training recovered what our quantisation pipeline cost the base".**

**H2b: +0.10, CI [−0.25, +0.45], reproduced.** Sign-flip p = 0.79. Harmful 8 vs 6, in the other direction.

**H1: 0/7, reproduced.**
- Two floors are missed by a single item: Spanish term 78.3% (19/23 would pass) and numbers 97.9%.
- B0-r meets 2/7 on v2.1 (numbers 99.1%, Spanish term 87.0%). On those two floors the trained model is under its base.

**H3: reproduced.**
- Spanish term at first use 4/22 = 18.2%; with the rubric's denominator of 23 rows it is 17.4%.
- Tails 2/30 for A12, R1 and R1+G alike; B0-r 4/30.

**H4: module range 1.0, reproduced.**
- B0-r and A12 both have a range of 0.4, so a 0.2 threshold is unattainable at 4–5 rows per module.
- Módulo 6, never trained, is R2's best module (1.6) and Módulo 1 its worst (0.6). That is not a leak signature.
- Non-context citations 4 vs 22, but R2 cites less overall: 79 vs 112 citations, on 41 vs 48 rows. The judge's unsupported-claim count is flat: 90 on 41 rows against 95 on 44.

**D1: 3/10 by count, but no comparison code exists in the record.**
- Greedy text differs from the first 60 characters on 8/10 rows; the extracted fields differ on 7/10.

**H5.**
- Run 2: Fable 2/40. Run 1: Fable 5/40, Opus 2/40. All reproduced.
- The 3-item change between runs equals the Fable–Opus disagreement on run 1. Run 2 had no second reader.

**Drift: 9/10, mean 1.00 vs 1.10, reproduced.** exp_038's 1.067 is not mentioned.

**Substance subset: 40 of 57 in-scope v1 rows; 17 excluded.** R2 − B0-r on the excluded rows is +0.18 (3 up, 0 down), so the subset does not manufacture the effect. The count is not reported in the write-up.

**Scope gate on v2.1:** A12 declined 0/2 out-of-scope rows; R1+G declined 1/2.
