The first arm failed two of three gates. Two amendments, each registered after the previous result and before its own run, passed all three.
- **An author's name in a question pulls the semantic ranking towards passages about the author, not only the lexical one.** Input: test, the one James question that failed, «Что Джеймс говорит о внимании?». Stripped of the name, the query ranks the chapter on attention first; with it, chapters on association, the stream of thought and the self. The same probe on «Что Мамардашвили говорит об амехании?» showed the raw semantic top ten led by the 1997 afterword and the 1995 editor's note, the passages about him; the lexical ranking had been carrying that question. Names are now stripped from both rankings.
- **A bilingual corpus is a dictionary: RU↔EN stem pairs aligned on Petukhov's own units give a Russian question lexical reach into English-only text.** Input: test, James's language drift of −0.56 passages per six between Russian and English questions, the 2026-10-28 drift mirrored. Pairs kept when two stems share units far more often than not (Dice ≥ 0.4, 4 shared units, mutual top 2): 6,176 links, «внимание» → attention, «привычка» → habit, «ощущение» → sensation, and associations where the term is a name («инстинкт» → instinct, McDougall). No model. Drift −0.56 → −0.40, within the registered bar; right in the number, wrong in the mechanism: English questions reached James less, their stems now meeting the Russian text of the other two corpora, and Russian ones did not reach him more.

**Learned (retrieval):**
- A registered bar can pass for the wrong reason. Report the per-corpus counts, not only the difference that gates.
- The 29-question harness passed every arm; only the new fixtures and the drift probe saw the failures. A harness built from the old log cannot see a new corpus.
